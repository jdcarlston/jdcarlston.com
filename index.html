WORKSHOP · EXPLORE DDD 2026

From EventStorm to Boundaries

A two-hour, hands-on workshop introducing my Domain Boundary Decision Model, the missing step after EventStorming. Three ideas carry the room:

  1. Every boundary serves an outside promise.
  2. Say how sure you are: firm, provisional, or not yet.
  3. Write down what would prove you wrong.
